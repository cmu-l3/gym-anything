# Task: Kitchen Remodel Planning

## Scenario

You own a 1,400 sq ft home built in 1978 in Charlotte, NC. The original kitchen (~120 sq ft) is tired: laminate counters, builder-grade cabinets, old appliances, and outdated layout. You want a full kitchen remodel — new cabinets, quartz countertops, appliances, flooring, and minor layout adjustment (remove a non-load-bearing half-wall). Your budget ceiling is **$45,000 all-in** (labor + materials + permits).

Research your project and **save your planning notes as a new Apple Notes note**.

## What to Include in the Note

The note should be detailed enough to walk into contractor meetings and make decisions. Cover at minimum:
- Average kitchen remodel cost range for a 120 sq ft kitchen in Charlotte, NC (mid-grade to high-end)
- Itemized cost breakdown: cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, labor, permits
- At least 3 Charlotte-area kitchen remodeling contractors with ratings/reviews and contact info
- Typical Charlotte permit requirements for a kitchen remodel (when is a permit required vs not)
- Cabinet options comparison: stock vs semi-custom vs custom, cost per linear foot
- Countertop material comparison: quartz vs granite vs butcher block, cost per sq ft installed
- Tips for staying under $45K: where to save vs splurge, common budget overruns
- Return on investment: typical kitchen remodel ROI in Charlotte's housing market

## Difficulty

**very_hard** — requires:
- Finding Charlotte-specific labor cost data (not national averages, which are 20–40% off)
- Sourcing local contractor reviews from Houzz, Angi, or HomeAdvisor for Charlotte specifically
- Understanding permit requirements for Charlotte-Mecklenburg (minor vs major kitchen work)
- Comparing cabinet and countertop options at multiple price points with installed costs
- Synthesizing cost data, contractor research, and permitting into a coherent plan under a hard budget

## Hardness Levers

1. Budget constraint: $45K hard ceiling requires real cost data, not aspirational estimates
2. Geography specificity: Charlotte labor rates differ from national figures
3. Multi-category research: cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, permits each from different sources
4. Contractor sourcing: must find actual Charlotte-area companies with reviews, not generic directories
5. Scope judgment: non-load-bearing wall removal adds permit complexity
6. ROI framing: must incorporate real estate impact in Charlotte market

## Verification Strategy

1. A fresh Apple Notes note exists (created/modified after task start)
2. A home improvement cost/contractor site was visited (houzz.com, angi.com, homeadvisor.com, thumbtack.com, homewyse.com)
3. A home improvement guide or retail site was visited (hgtv.com, thisoldhouse.com, bobvila.com, lowes.com, homedepot.com, ikea.com)
4. Note has substantial content length
5. Note contains relevant domain keywords (cabinet, countertop, permit, contractor, labor, quartz, appliance, budget, remodel)
6. Note is comprehensive (both length and keyword thresholds)

**Pass threshold**: 70/100
