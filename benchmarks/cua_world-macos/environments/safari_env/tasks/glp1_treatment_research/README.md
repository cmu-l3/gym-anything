# Task: GLP-1 Medication Research for Doctor Appointment

## Scenario

Your doctor just told you that you qualify for a GLP-1 receptor agonist medication for type 2 diabetes and weight management. She mentioned three options: Ozempic (semaglutide), Mounjaro (tirzepatide), and Wegovy (semaglutide higher dose). You have a follow-up appointment in two weeks and want to arrive prepared with questions and data.

Research these medications and **save your findings as a new note in Apple Notes** to bring to your appointment.

## What to Include in the Note

The note should be detailed enough to have an informed conversation with your doctor. Cover at minimum:
- How each drug works (mechanism) and approved indications
- Key clinical trial results: weight loss %, HbA1c reduction, cardiovascular benefit evidence
- Common and serious side effects to ask about
- Insurance coverage notes (Medicare Part D, typical commercial coverage)
- Approximate out-of-pocket costs with and without insurance/manufacturer coupons
- How they're administered (injection frequency, pen vs vial)
- Any specific questions to ask your doctor based on your research

## Difficulty

**very_hard** — requires:
- Finding accurate clinical trial data across multiple drug comparison sites and medical references
- Understanding cardiovascular outcome trial (CVOT) results (SUSTAIN-6, LEADER, SURPASS-CVOT, SELECT) at a lay level
- Sourcing current insurance/formulary information and manufacturer savings programs
- Comparing three drugs that differ in mechanism, dose, and approved indications
- Synthesizing medical literature from NIH/PubMed and consumer-level sources

## Hardness Levers

1. Multi-drug comparison: 3 drugs with overlapping but distinct clinical profiles
2. Medical accuracy: trial names, endpoints, % outcomes must be correct
3. Financial reality: insurance coverage and costs vary; savings cards have eligibility restrictions
4. Source diversity: clinical evidence (PubMed/NIH) + prescribing info + consumer cost sites
5. Personal relevance: patient-perspective framing, what to ask the doctor
6. Rapidly changing field: GLP-1 approvals expanded significantly 2021–2024

## Verification Strategy

1. A fresh Apple Notes note exists (created/modified after task start)
2. A clinical/medical information site was visited (drugs.com, mayoclinic.org, medlineplus.gov, ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, nejm.org, diabetes.org)
3. A cost/coverage site was visited (goodrx.com, needymeds.org, rxsaver.com, drugs.com/price-guide)
4. Note has substantial content length
5. Note contains relevant domain keywords (ozempic, semaglutide, trial, weight, coverage, insulin, cardiovascular, diabetes, side)
6. Note is comprehensive (both length and keyword thresholds)

**Pass threshold**: 70/100
