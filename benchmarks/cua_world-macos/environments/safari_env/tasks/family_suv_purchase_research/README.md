# Task: Family SUV Purchase Research

## Scenario

Your family of 5 in Marietta, GA is replacing your 2018 Honda CR-V (68,000 miles) that has become too small. You need a 3-row SUV with seating for 6+, towing capacity ≥3,500 lbs for a camping trailer, max out-the-door price $45,000 after trade-in (~$18,000 for the CR-V), ADAS features (blind-spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert), and good lumbar support (one driver has chronic back problems).

Research and compare at least 4 specific 2024 or 2025 model-year 3-row SUVs. **Save your comparison as a new note in Apple Notes** so you and your partner can review it before visiting dealerships.

## What to Include in the Note

The note should be detailed enough to walk into a dealership prepared. Cover at minimum:
- At least 4 specific models with year, base MSRP, and typical transaction price
- Towing capacity and third-row cargo space for each
- ADAS features standard on each (or required trim level)
- Reliability ratings for each, with source (Consumer Reports, J.D. Power, etc.)
- Lumbar support and seat adjustability notes — which trim level is needed?
- Nearest dealer to Marietta, GA for each brand
- Monthly payment estimate for each at 6.9% APR, 60-month term, after $18,000 trade-in

You decide how to organize the note — it's your personal car-buying research.

## Difficulty

**very_hard** — requires:
- Synthesizing MSRP data from pricing sites with reliability ratings from separate consumer testing organizations
- Calculating monthly payment math (principal = transaction price − $18,000; amortize at 6.9%/12 per month for 60 months)
- Finding actual dealer inventory near Marietta, GA (not just the brand name)
- Checking lumbar support specifics which vary by trim level
- Comparing towing specs and cargo space across competing models

## Hardness Levers

1. Household budget: $45,000 OTD constraint with trade-in math
2. Multi-source synthesis: pricing sites + reliability orgs + dealer locators + specs
3. Personal preferences: lumbar support for chronic back issue
4. Multi-stakeholder: camping family needs real towing capacity
5. Real-world specificity: actual dealer names near Marietta; actual MSRP and specs
6. Financial math: monthly payment must be calculated correctly

## Verification Strategy

1. A fresh Apple Notes note exists (created/modified after task start)
2. A vehicle pricing/comparison site was visited (edmunds, cars.com, kbb, carmax, caranddriver, motortrend, cargurus, autotrader)
3. A reliability/review site was visited (consumerreports, jdpower, motortrend)
4. Note has substantial content length
5. Note contains relevant domain keywords (suv, msrp, towing, payment, reliability, dealer, etc.)
6. Note is comprehensive (both length and keyword thresholds)

**Pass threshold**: 70/100
