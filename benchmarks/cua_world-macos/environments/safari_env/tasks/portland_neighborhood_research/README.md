# Task: Portland Neighborhood Research for Family Relocation

## Scenario

Your family is relocating from Dallas, TX to the Portland, OR metro area for a new job at Intel's Hillsboro campus (Jones Farm, 2111 NE 25th Ave, Hillsboro OR 97124). You have two kids: a 10-year-old with an ADHD diagnosis (requiring IEP/504 accommodations) and a 16-year-old interested in theater and robotics. Your household budget is either **$650,000 to buy** or **$3,000/month max to rent**.

Research and compare at least **3 specific Portland-area neighborhoods** as potential places to live. **Save your comparison as a new note in Apple Notes** so you and your partner can review it before house-hunting trips.

## What to Include in the Note

The note should be detailed enough to walk into a real estate meeting prepared. Cover at minimum:
- At least 3 specific neighborhoods with housing costs (median buy price and 2BR rent)
- For each: an elementary school and high school with ratings and source
- For each elementary school: IEP/504/SPED support notes for the district
- For the 16-year-old: which high schools have theater and/or robotics programs
- Commute time/mode from each neighborhood to Intel Jones Farm (Hillsboro)
- Walkability and parks/rec notes for each neighborhood
- A summary of commute options (MAX Blue Line, Intel shuttle, etc.)

## Difficulty

**very_hard** — requires:
- Finding current school-level GreatSchools or Niche ratings for specific elementary and high schools
- Researching SPED/IEP/504 program availability at the district level — not just "district has SPED" but specific support structures
- Locating high school extracurricular programs (theater, robotics) which vary by campus
- Sourcing current (2024/2025) median home prices and rental rates per neighborhood (not metro-wide averages)
- Calculating/verifying drive time from each neighborhood to Intel Jones Farm specifically (not just "near Portland")
- Synthesizing all of this across 3+ geographically distinct neighborhoods

## Hardness Levers

1. Multi-stakeholder: 10-year-old needs IEP/504 support; 16-year-old needs specific extracurriculars
2. Household budget: hard ceiling of $650K buy / $3K/month rent
3. Geography constraint: commute must be to a specific campus (Jones Farm, Hillsboro)
4. Multi-source synthesis: school ratings (GreatSchools/Niche) + district SPED info + real estate (Zillow/Redfin) + commute (Google Maps)
5. Real-world specificity: actual school names, actual district SPED policies, actual current prices
6. Personal preferences: accessibility needs + teen extracurriculars
7. Conflicting information: school ratings vary by source; some neighborhoods straddle district boundaries

## Verification Strategy

1. A fresh Apple Notes note exists (created/modified after task start)
2. A real estate site was visited (zillow, redfin, realtor.com, apartments.com, trulia)
3. A school research site was visited (greatschools.org, niche.com, schooldigger.com, district sites)
4. Note has substantial content length
5. Note contains relevant domain keywords (school, neighborhood, commute, district, rent, etc.)
6. Note is comprehensive (both length and keyword thresholds)

**Pass threshold**: 70/100
