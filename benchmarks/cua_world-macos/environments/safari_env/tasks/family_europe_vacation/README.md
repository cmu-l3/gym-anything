# Task: Family Europe Vacation Planning

## Scenario

You and your partner are planning your first major family vacation abroad — a 14-day trip through Western Europe in July with your two children (ages 8 and 14). Your total budget for the entire trip is $12,000 (flights from New York JFK, accommodation, activities, and food for all four people). Your partner recently had hip surgery and cannot walk more than 2–3 miles per day and cannot manage stairs without a handrail. Your 8-year-old loses interest quickly in museums without hands-on components. You want to cover at least three countries or distinct regions.

Research flights, accommodation, and activities for **at least 3 European cities**, then **save your itinerary plan as a new note in Apple Notes** so you can share it with your family.

## What to Include in the Note

The note should be genuinely useful for planning — detailed enough that your family could use it to prepare for the trip. Cover at minimum:
- Which cities/countries and why (brief rationale)
- Flight options and approximate costs from JFK
- Hotel or apartment options with approximate nightly rates and accessibility notes
- 3+ activities per city that work for both your 8-year-old and 14-year-old, and are accessible for someone with limited mobility
- Inter-city transport between stops
- Rough daily food budget per person
- Any specific accessibility notes for major attractions (lifts, cobblestone areas, etc.)

You decide how to organize the note — it's your personal planning document.

## Difficulty

**very_hard** — requires multi-source synthesis across flight booking sites, accommodation platforms, and attraction databases, combined with:
- **Mobility constraint** (partner's hip surgery) must be reflected in accommodation and activity choices
- **Age-differentiated** activities: 8-year-old needs hands-on/active; 14-year-old can handle more adult options
- **Budget math**: total estimated cost under $12,000 for 4 people over 14 days
- **3+ distinct countries or regions** requiring inter-city logistics

## Hardness Levers

1. Multi-stakeholder: 4 family members with conflicting needs (8-year-old, 14-year-old, partner with limited mobility)
2. Household budget: $12,000 hard cap covering all expenses
3. Geography: 3+ countries requiring inter-city travel planning
4. Personal preferences: mobility impairment is a hard constraint, not optional
5. Multi-source synthesis: flights + accommodation + activities + transport from different sites
6. Output artifact integrity: note must be genuinely detailed and organized

## Verification Strategy

1. A fresh Apple Notes note was created/modified after task start
2. A flight/travel comparison site was visited (expedia, kayak, skyscanner, google/travel)
3. An accommodation site was visited (booking, airbnb, hotels, vrbo)
4. An activity/travel review site was visited (tripadvisor, viator, lonelyplanet, getyourguide)
5. Note has substantial content length
6. Note contains relevant domain keywords (flights, hotel, accessible, activities, budget, etc.)

**Pass threshold**: 70/100
